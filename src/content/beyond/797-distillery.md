Rashelle and Darren Chaney announced 797 Distillery in a historic Covington building that once ran as a Coca-Cola bottling plant, near the town's Art Depot District. The plan was a tasting room and a lineup of moonshine, bourbon, rum and brandy, "small in size, but big in flavor."

We haven't confirmed that the tasting room is open yet. If you've been, [tell us](/contact/).
