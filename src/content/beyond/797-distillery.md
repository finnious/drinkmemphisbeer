Darren and Rashelle Chaney built 797 Distillery in a historic Covington building near the town's Art Depot District, and the tasting room is now open on weekends.

The lineup is small-batch moonshine, vodka and rum, distilled in copper pot stills from Tipton County grain, some of it grown on the family's farm in Burlison. Bottles are sold at the distillery and at select West Tennessee retailers.

The distillery makes a point of Covington's water. We'd like to note, respectfully, that Memphis has the [Memphis Sand Aquifer](/water/), and we're not worried.
