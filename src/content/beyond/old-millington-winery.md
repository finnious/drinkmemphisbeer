Perry and Carrie Welch planted vines on their Millington land after a trip to Napa, and opened Old Millington Winery in 2000. It's a small country operation just 14 miles north of downtown Memphis, and the closest winery to the city.

They grow Chambourcin grapes for their reds and also make whites, blushes, ports and fruit wines. Names like Delta White, Big River Red and Crying Angel Red give it a local flavor. The wine is sold only at the winery.

**Visiting:** live music runs on the outdoor stage most Sunday afternoons from spring into fall, with a break for the July and August heat. Check with the winery for current hours.
