In 1866 Domenico Canale started a Memphis business that included a whiskey named for his nickname, "Old Dominick." Five generations later, cousins Chris and Alex Canale brought the brand back in 2017, in a 50,000-square-foot set of restored warehouses on Front Street, a few blocks from Beale.

It's the first distillery making whiskey in downtown Memphis since before Prohibition. When Old Dominick Straight Tennessee Whiskey was released, it became the first whiskey distilled, aged and bottled in the city since Prohibition.

The lineup includes Tennessee whiskey, bourbon, Memphis vodka, Honeybell citrus vodka, gin, and the Memphis Toddy, a revived pre-Prohibition whiskey liqueur from the family archives. In 2026 its Memphis Vodka scored 95 points at the Bartender Spirits Awards.

**Visiting:** tours end with a tasting, and there's a restaurant on site, so it works as a full evening downtown.
