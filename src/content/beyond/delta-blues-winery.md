Turn off Highway 385 onto Stewart Road and look for the black iron gates. The drive winds past a small lake with the vineyard growing around it, down to the tasting room at the bottom of the slope.

Delta Blues is one of three West Tennessee wineries in the Mississippi Delta wine region (a federally recognized grape-growing area), along with Old Millington and de terra. It has indoor and outdoor seating, and reservations are welcome. Check the winery's website for current hours.
