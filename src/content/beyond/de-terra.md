de terra sits on 34 acres in Somerville, the Fayette County seat, about four miles off I-40 at exit 42. It makes red, white and fruit wines and is a stop on the Wine Trail of West Tennessee.

Somerville itself is worth the trip: a small courthouse square with cafés and shops, less than an hour from Memphis. Make a day of it with lunch on the square and a tasting at de terra. The winery also hosts events through the year, including its Grapevine Jubilee.
