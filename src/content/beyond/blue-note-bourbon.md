B.R. Distilling Company, originally Big River Distilling, was founded in Memphis in 2014. That makes it the city's oldest licensed distillery, even though Old Dominick's brand is older.

Its flagship is Blue Note Bourbon, named for Memphis blues. The core lineup is Juke Joint (the everyday bottle), Crossroads and Uncut, all non-chill-filtered. A Small Batch Wheated Bourbon Blend joined in April 2026.

**Visiting:** guided behind-the-scenes tours with a tasting run Thursdays and Fridays and end in a speakeasy-style tasting room. Book through the Blue Note website. You must be 21+ for the tasting.
