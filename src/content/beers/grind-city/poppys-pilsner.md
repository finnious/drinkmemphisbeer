Poppy's Pils is a light American pilsner fermented with a Mexican lager yeast, so it lands somewhere between a crisp pils and a beach lager. Memphis Beer Blog calls it the Grind City beer you need to try, and it's the obvious order in the yard at 76 Waterworks Ave.
