The Godhopper is Grind City's hazy IPA, 6.5% and built on a big dose of fruit-forward hops. It's one of the Uptown brewery's year-round beers, and a good one to take out to the lawn by Wolf River Harbor.

Grind City left the wholesale market in 2025 to focus on taproom sales, so the taproom is the place to find it.
