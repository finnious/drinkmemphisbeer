Tan Lines is a Kölsch with tangerine from Memphis Made. The brewery closed its last taproom in March 2026, and in June Soul & Spirits bought the Memphis Made brand, promising to keep its beers in stores and on tap. The Flyer's report named Fireside Amber but not Tan Lines, so we're not sure this one made the jump. If you've seen it lately, [tell us](/contact/).
