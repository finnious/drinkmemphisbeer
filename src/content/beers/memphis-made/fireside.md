Fireside is a red ale built on Munich malt with European hops: malty and a little roasty up front, balanced at the finish. Memphis Travel calls it one of the most guzzled beers in the city, and you'll still find it on draft around town, including at the airport. Memphis Made now operates under Soul & Spirits.
