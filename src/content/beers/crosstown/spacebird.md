Spacebird is a sweet, donut-inspired stout at 5.2%, a regular sight on Memphis tap lists.

Crosstown Brewing closed its taproom for good on October 17, 2026. Its distributor said draft Crosstown beer would stay on at local bars and restaurants until supply runs out, so if you spot this one on a tap list, it may be one of the last kegs.
