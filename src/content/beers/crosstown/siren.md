Siren is Crosstown's go-anywhere beer. It's a blonde ale hopped with Hallertauer Mittelfrüh, so there's a little spice and some floral, citrusy notes, but the bitterness stays low. It's the one to hand a friend who says they "don't really like beer."

Crosstown Brewing closed its taproom for good on October 17, 2026. Its distributor said draft Crosstown beer would stay on at local bars and restaurants until supply runs out, so if you spot this one on a tap list, it may be one of the last kegs.
