Pizza Whisperer is an American amber ale and one of Rock'n Dough's most-logged beers on Untappd. Caramel malt and a modest hop finish make it a natural match for a pie, which is the point of the name. Rock'n Dough's tap list rotates and its beers are brewed across its locations, so check the board at the one you're visiting.
