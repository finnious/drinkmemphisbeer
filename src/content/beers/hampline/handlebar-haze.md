Handlebar Haze is Hampline's New England-style IPA and one of the first beers the Binghampton brewery put on Untappd, back in early 2021. It's hoppy without much bitterness, with tropical fruit on the nose and the signature haze. At 6.6% it's the middle of Hampline's hazy family: Crazy Chain is lighter and wheatier, and Tandem Pilot is the double.
