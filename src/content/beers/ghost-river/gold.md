Gold is Ghost River's oldest regular and still its flagship: a clean golden ale with a dry finish. Ghost River has always made a point of brewing with Memphis' aquifer water, which makes this one a natural first pour on the Tap Water Test.

It's 6% with very little bitterness (11 IBU), built to go anywhere.
