201 Hoplar is the beer that made Meddlesome. The Cordova brewery opened in 2017, and 201 Hoplar won the Memphis Flyer's Beer Bracket Challenge the next year, then again the two years after that. Readers voted it past Wiseacre's Tiny Bomb in the 2018 Final Four.

It's a West Coast IPA, bitter and resinous with pine, grapefruit and pineapple from Chinook and Columbus hops.
