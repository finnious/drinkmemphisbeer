Dunkel Puppy is a dunkelweizen, a dark German wheat beer, at 5.4%. Wiseacre's own shorthand is chocolate banana bread: the wheat yeast brings banana and clove, and the darker malts add cocoa and toast. Easy drinking for a beer that looks heavy.
