Wiseacre's Oktoberfest is a traditional Märzen lager at 6.2%. The brewery's own note is that it's still over 80 degrees in Memphis when Oktoberfest rolls around, so they made it an easy-drinking malt bomb instead of a heavy one. Look for it in the fall.
