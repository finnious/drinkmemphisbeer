Future Ancestor is Wiseacre's take on a Tennessee common, a corn lager in the old pre-Prohibition Southern tradition. It's 5% and 15 IBU, so expect a clean, slightly sweet corn character rather than hops. A nice companion to the history on our [history page](/history/).
