Beach Within Reach is Wiseacre's Berliner weisse: tart, light and fizzy, which the brewery sums up as champagne-like. It shows up in variations too, including peach, watermelon, apricot and a Black Forest version. At 4.2% it's a summer beer for a city that stays hot into October.
