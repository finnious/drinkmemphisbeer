Tiny Bomb was one of the two beers Wiseacre opened with in 2013. Davin Bartosch picked up his love of pilsner in brewing school in Chicago, and a year after launch the beer won bronze in the German-Style Pilsener category at the Great American Beer Festival.

It's a light, crisp, golden lager at 4.5%, which makes it the default patio and ballgame beer for a lot of Memphis. It's also the biggest craft beer brand in Tennessee.
