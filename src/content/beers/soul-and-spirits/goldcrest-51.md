Goldcrest 51 was the beer Memphis drank for decades. The Tennessee Brewery on the bluff made it until it closed in 1954. In February 2024, Jake Schorr, great-grandson of the brewery's founder J.W. Schorr and owner of Westy's, brought it back with Soul & Spirits brewmaster Ryan Allen.

It's an American-style lager: cracker malt, a little sweet corn, a light touch of spice. The Memphis Flyer reported in 2026 that the revival was still going strong two years in. Read the full story on our [history page](/history/).
