Like most Soul & Spirits beers, Hoochie Coochie takes its name from a blues record, in this case Muddy Waters' "Hoochie Coochie Man." It's a dank, full-bodied American IPA. Published ABVs disagree (Untappd lists 6.3%, BeerMenus 7.1%), so we've left the number off until we check a can.
