Bring It Home is Soul & Spirits' take on a Bavarian helles: a soft, golden lager at 4.9%. Craft Beer & Brewing's panel scored it a 96, one of the highest marks we've seen for a Memphis beer. It's the one to start with at 845 N. Main.
