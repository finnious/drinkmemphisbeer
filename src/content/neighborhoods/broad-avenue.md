---
name: Broad Avenue
summary: Arts district in Binghampton with Wiseacre's original taproom and Hampline, linked by bike path to Overton Park.
---

Broad Avenue is a walkable arts strip in Binghampton. It has two breweries: Wiseacre's original taproom and Hampline, tucked just off Broad. The Hampline bike path connects it all to Overton Park.
