---
name: Germantown
summary: East Memphis suburb where Tennessee's first brewpub, Boscos, opened in 1992. Today it has Rock'n Dough's brewpub on Poplar.
---

Germantown has a bigger place in Tennessee beer history than most people know. Boscos opened here in 1992 as the state's first brewpub, and its founders helped change Tennessee's outdated beer laws. Today Rock'n Dough's Poplar Avenue brewpub carries the torch.
