---
name: Downtown
summary: Beale Street, FedExForum and the river, with a brewery taproom within walking distance of the Grizzlies.
---

Downtown is where most visitors stay, so it's where most brewery crawls start. Wiseacre's downtown taproom on B.B. King Blvd and Ghost River's Beale Street taproom are both short walks from FedExForum, and Memphis Filling Station is on G.E. Patterson near South Main.
