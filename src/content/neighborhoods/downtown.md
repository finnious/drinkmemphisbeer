name: Downtown
summary: Beale Street, FedExForum and the river, with a brewery taproom within walking distance of the Grizzlies.
---

Downtown is where most visitors stay, so it's where most brewery crawls start. Wiseacre's downtown taproom on B.B. King Blvd is a short walk from FedExForum and Beale Street, and South Main and the Edge District are each a quick ride away.
