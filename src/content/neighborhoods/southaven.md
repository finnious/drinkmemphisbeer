---
name: Southaven & DeSoto County
summary: Just south of the state line, now Memphis's largest suburb. Breweries are scarce here, which makes each one worth the drive.
---

Southaven passed Bartlett in 2024 to become Memphis's largest suburb by population. Mississippi came late to craft beer, so DeSoto County has only a handful of breweries. See also [Olive Branch](/neighborhoods/olive-branch/).
