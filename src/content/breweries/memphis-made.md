Memphis Made was one of the city's more established breweries and a Cooper-Young anchor. It filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy on August 7, 2025, about a year after closing its original brewery, and closed its second taproom on March 29, 2026.

Its cans lingered on store shelves for a while after, including Tan Lines, a Kölsch with tangerine. If you still have one in the fridge, you're holding a piece of Memphis beer history.
