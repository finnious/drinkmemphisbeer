Memphis Made was one of the city's more established breweries and a Cooper-Young anchor. It filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy on August 7, 2025, about a year after leaving its original Cooper-Young brewery for a new home on S. Lauderdale St in the Edge District. That taproom closed on March 29, 2026.

It was known for beers like Fireside amber, Junt cream ale and Cat Nap IPA. In 2024 the Flyer counted it as the fourth-largest producing brewery in Memphis.

The beer didn't die with the taproom. In June 2026 [Soul & Spirits](/breweries/soul-and-spirits/) bought the Memphis Made trademark and said it would keep the brand's beers in stores and on tap, with Memphis Made on draft and to-go at its Uptown taproom. Fireside Amber, the fan favorite, is the one to look for.
