You can't visit it, but you've probably driven past it. The plant on East Raines Road in southeast Memphis was built by Schlitz in 1971 and later owned by Coors. In 2011 it was bought by City Brewing, a company with breweries in La Crosse, Wisconsin; Latrobe, Pennsylvania; Irwindale, California; and Memphis.

Today it's a contract brewery, making drinks for other companies' brands rather than its own taproom beers. It's listed here because it's a big part of the city's brewing history, and because Coors once chose Memphis for the quality of its water.
