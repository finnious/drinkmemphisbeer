Boscos is where modern craft beer in Tennessee started. Businessman Jerry Feinstone and brewer Chuck Skypeck opened it in 1992 in the Saddle Creek shopping center in Germantown, as the state's first brewpub, and helped change Tennessee's outdated beer laws along the way.

Its Memphis home is a full restaurant with a patio on Madison Avenue near Overton Square, with beer brewed on site. Boscos was still pouring at the 2026 Cooper-Young Beerfest, but we haven't confirmed the current taproom hours. If you've been recently, [tell us](/contact/).
