Void Brew Co. is a nanobrewery in Southaven, Mississippi, and by its own description it is "still becoming what it will be." It has no taproom yet. The brewers say they're building toward a permanent one in Southaven, and they take donations for equipment, licensing and ingredients to get there.

Until then, Void pours at events. It was on the 2026 Cooper-Young Beerfest lineup, which is how most Memphians will have met it.

## What to drink

Void's beers are rarely permanent, which is the whole idea. Recent batches on Untappd include Mystic River, an apple-and-caramel cider at 6.5% ABV; Bonfire Buddies, a milk stout; and There Is Comfort in the Dark.

When the taproom opens, it'll be one of the only breweries in DeSoto County, and we'll update this page. If you hear first, [tell us](/contact/).
