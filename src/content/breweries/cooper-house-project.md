Cooper House Project is exactly what the name says: a brewery in an ordinary-looking house on Cooper Street in Cooper-Young. Owner and brewer Travis Wiseman skips the flagship model entirely. Small batches rotate in and out, and they're sold only in the taproom.

The name comes from a cooper, the craftsman who makes and repairs casks and barrels. The brewery focuses on beer aged in wood, modified traditional styles, historic beers, and wild and sour ales and lagers.

It's also a coffee house and pizza spot, and the taproom doubles as a gallery, showing local and regional artists on a rotating basis. It's the coziest stop on any Cooper-Young walk.
