Flyway Brewing, based in Little Rock, bought the old [High Cotton](/breweries/high-cotton/) location at 598 Monroe Avenue. The taproom now mixes Flyway's own beers with High Cotton recipes, so the High Cotton name lives on.

Flyway's Bluewing is a berry wheat ale.
