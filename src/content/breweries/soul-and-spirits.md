Soul & Spirits is in Uptown, just north of downtown, in a big taproom where dogs are welcome inside. The Memphis Flyer singles it out as one of the city's most creative and experimental breweries.

Its Bring It Home is a Bavarian-style helles lager.

In June 2026 Soul & Spirits bought the [Memphis Made](/breweries/memphis-made/) brand and said it would keep Memphis Made beers in stores and on tap, including on draft and to-go at this taproom.
