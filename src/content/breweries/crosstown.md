Crosstown Brewing opened in 2018 in its own building near Crosstown Concourse, the old Sears building turned into a vertical village. It's an easy add-on to a Concourse visit, with seating inside and out.

One of its best-known beers is Fool's Gold, an American lager brewed with Huey's, the Memphis burger institution.
