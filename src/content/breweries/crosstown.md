Crosstown Brewing opened its taproom on February 16, 2018, in its own building near Crosstown Concourse, the old Sears building turned into a vertical village. Will Goodwin and Clark Ortkiese founded it, and its beer was distributed in Tennessee, Mississippi and Arkansas.

The taproom poured for the last time on Saturday, October 17, 2026. That day's Crosstoberfest became the farewell party. Goodwin told the Commercial Appeal that "financial reasons" were behind the closing and that it had been "a fairly long time coming."

The company said its draft beer would stay on at local restaurants and bars until supply runs out, so you may still spot Traffic or Siren on a tap list for a little while. Its best-known beers also included Fool's Gold, an American lager brewed with Huey's.
