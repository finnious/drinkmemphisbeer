Rock'n Dough started as a food truck slinging pizza around Memphis, then grew into brick-and-mortar restaurants and its own brewery operation in Jackson, Tennessee.

The Germantown location became a brewpub, pouring its own house-brewed beer. There are now three Memphis-area spots: Germantown on Poplar, Cordova on North Germantown Parkway, and the Edge District on Madison, which adds a patio, bar and duckpin bowling lanes.

It's the easy answer when half the group wants beer and the other half wants pizza.
