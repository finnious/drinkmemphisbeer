Hampline is named for the Hampline, the bike and walking path that links Broad Avenue to Overton Park. The taproom is small, with a patio, and sits behind the Rec Room just off Broad.

Ride the Hampline in, have a beer, and you've earned it. Handlebar Haze, a New England-style IPA, is one of its regulars.
