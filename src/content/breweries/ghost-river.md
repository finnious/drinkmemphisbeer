Ghost River takes its name from a section of the Wolf River that serves as an entrance to the Memphis Aquifer, the same aquifer that supplies the city's tap water. No brewery in town has a better claim to the water story.

It was also the first brewery in Memphis since Prohibition, which makes it the starting point for the city's modern beer scene.

Ghost River has two taprooms: the original on South Main, close to the National Civil Rights Museum, and one on Beale Street.
