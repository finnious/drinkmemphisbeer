Grind City is the outdoor brewery: a large yard plus indoor seating on Waterworks Avenue. It has pulled its cans from store shelves and turned its attention to the taproom and an amphitheater for concerts, which was set to open in spring 2026.

On tap you'll find beers like Poppy's Pilsner and Godhopper, a hazy IPA.
