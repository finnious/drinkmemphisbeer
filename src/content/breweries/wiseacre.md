Wiseacre is the brewery most people outside Memphis already know. Brothers Kellan and Davin Bartosch opened the doors in 2013 with just two beers: Ananda, an IPA, and Tiny Bomb, an American pilsner. About a year later, Tiny Bomb won a bronze medal at the Great American Beer Festival.

Tiny Bomb still makes up nearly half of what the brewery produces. In mid-2025 Wiseacre bought Nashville's Bearded Iris, whose Homestyle IPA is now brewed in Memphis too.

There are two taprooms. The original on Broad Avenue is the neighborhood hangout. The downtown taproom on B.B. King Blvd opened in 2020, a short walk from FedExForum, and has a sculpture garden and pizza.
