Memphis Filling Station, also called MFS Brewing, is one of the newest breweries downtown. Its taproom on G.E. Patterson opened in 2024, a short walk from South Main.

The Memphis Flyer's May 2026 guide named it, along with Meddlesome and Soul & Spirits, among the city's most creative and experimental brewers.
