Beale St. Brewing launched in July 2020 as Memphis' first Black-owned brewery. Founder Kelvin Kolheim was director of economic development at the Greater Memphis Chamber before he started brewing, and he has said he sees the brewery as the same job in different shoes: an ambassador for Memphis.

There is no Beale St. Brewing building to visit. The beer is contract-brewed at Lazy Magnolia in Kiln, Mississippi, and sold in cans in Memphis. In 2023 it signed with Clark Beverage Group to reach most of Mississippi north of Jackson, including DeSoto County, Oxford, Clarksdale and Tupelo.

## What to drink

Space Age Sippin, a hazy IPA, is the one you'll see most. King's Ransom is the other regular. A Black History Month series put Memphis figures on the cans, including Mayor Willie Herenton, Robert R. Church, Memphis Minnie and Charley Pride.

When Beale St. put out a call for a citywide collaboration brew, Wiseacre, Crosstown, Meddlesome, Memphis Made, Ghost River, Soul & Spirits and Memphis Filling Station all signed on.
