Mississippi Ale House opened in Olive Branch in 2017 with 11 taps and one rule: only beer brewed in Mississippi. It grew to 30 taps a year later and loosened up enough to pour regional beer too, but the point is still a tour of the state by the flight.

In 2022 the Ale House bought Backwater Brewing, the cidery next door, and started brewing under its own name. The house beers include Ale House Light, Arm Chair Amber and Hop Doctor IPA, plus the Pineapple Deception cider carried over from Backwater. Specialty beers reach Memphis stores under a "Brewlab" label.

## Good to know

There's no kitchen. Bring food in, or walk next door to Sidestreet Burgers. Trivia, karaoke and live music run weekly, and the Ale House throws a Brewfest every spring with 200-plus beers from 50-plus breweries.

It's about 25 minutes from Midtown, and yes, it counts. The Memphis Flyer includes it in the area's brewery count, and so do we.
