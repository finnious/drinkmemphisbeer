Maroon Brew Co. is Collierville's hometown brewpub. Owner and head brewer Maggie Emerson left the software industry to brew, and she runs it with her family.

The name is a nod to the town: maroon is Collierville's school color (and, she points out, the color of its recycling bins). Many of the beers are named for Collierville landmarks.

It's family-friendly and all ages are welcome, with food on the menu, so it's an easy pick for a group that isn't all beer people.
