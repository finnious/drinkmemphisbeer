Meddlesome is one of the few Memphis-area breweries outside downtown and Midtown. It's in Cordova, just past Shelby Farms Park, so it pairs well with a day on the park's trails.

The slogan is "never settle, always meddle", and the Memphis Flyer counts it among the city's most experimental brewers. The taproom has indoor and outdoor seating.
