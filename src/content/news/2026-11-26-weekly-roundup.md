Happy Thanksgiving. We looked around and found no Memphis brewery openings, closings, new releases or festival dates announced this week. Rather than pad it out, here are two updates we should have had on the site already.

**Crosstown Brewing has closed.** The taproom on Concourse Avenue poured for the last time on Saturday, October 17, after eight years, with that day's Crosstoberfest doubling as the farewell. Founder Will Goodwin told the Commercial Appeal it came down to "financial reasons." The company said its draft beer would stay on at local bars and restaurants until it runs out, so a stray keg of Traffic or Siren may still turn up. The Daily Memphian's AM/DM podcast took a wider look this month at why Memphis taprooms keep pulling back. [Crosstown's page](/breweries/crosstown/) and its beers are now marked closed and retired.

**Memphis Made beer is still around.** Back in June, Soul & Spirits bought the Memphis Made brand and promised to keep its beers in stores and on tap, including at its own Uptown taproom. So if Fireside Amber was your beer, it's not gone. [Memphis Made's page](/breweries/memphis-made/) now points to [Soul & Spirits](/breweries/soul-and-spirits/).

**A tip for the long weekend:** call or check a brewery's social pages before you head over on Thanksgiving Day or Black Friday. Holiday hours change, and nobody wants to drive across town to a locked door. The [Status Board](/status/) shows when we last checked each brewery. If a place turns out to be closed, Memphis tap water is still pouring, and it's still the bar every beer here has to clear.
