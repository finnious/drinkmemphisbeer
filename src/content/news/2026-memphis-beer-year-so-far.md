It's been a rough year for craft beer nationally, and Memphis has felt it. Here's the short version before the weekly roundups start.

**Memphis Made lost its taproom.** The Cooper-Young brewery filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in August 2025 and closed its second taproom on March 29, 2026. In June, Soul & Spirits bought the Memphis Made brand and is keeping its beers going. [Memphis Made's page](/breweries/memphis-made/) has the story.

**High Cotton became Flyway.** Little Rock's Flyway Brewing took over the old High Cotton taproom on Monroe Avenue and pours both breweries' recipes. [See Flyway.](/breweries/flyway/)

**Grind City pivoted.** It stopped selling cans in stores and is putting its energy into the taproom and its outdoor concert venue. [See Grind City.](/breweries/grind-city/)

**Crosstown Brewing closed in October.** Its taproom poured for the last time on October 17, after eight years. [See Crosstown.](/breweries/crosstown/)

**Hook Point and Urban Consequence are closed.** The Memphis Flyer counts four breweries from the post-2013 boom that didn't survive.

**Wiseacre got bigger.** It bought Nashville's Bearded Iris in mid-2025, and Bearded Iris's Homestyle IPA is now brewed in Memphis.

**The good news:** Cooper House Project, Flyway, Ghost River, Hampline, Maroon Brew, Meddlesome, Memphis Filling Station, Soul & Spirits and Wiseacre are all pouring. The [Status Board](/status/) shows every brewery, open or closed, with the date we last checked.
